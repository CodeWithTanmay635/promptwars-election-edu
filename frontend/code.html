Who can vote in the national elections?
Every Indian citizen who is 18 years of age or older on the qualifying date is eligible to be registered as a voter in the electoral roll. They must be an ordinary resident in the constituency and not disqualified under any law.

The Election Lifecycle

Tracking the journey from announcement to results

1

Official Notification

Day 0

The Election Commission issues the formal notification announcing the election schedule and key dates.

2

Candidate Nomination

7-10 Days

Candidates file their nomination papers, including affidavits regarding their criminal record, assets, and liabilities.

3

Scrutiny & Withdrawal

3 Days

ECI officials verify nomination papers. Candidates have a window to withdraw their names if they choose.

4

Campaigning Period

14+ Days

Political parties and candidates engage in rallies and outreach under the Model Code of Conduct guidelines.

5

Pre-Poll Silence

48 Hours

All campaigning must cease 48 hours before the conclusion of the poll to allow voters time to reflect.

6

Polling Day

Actual Date

Citizens visit assigned polling stations to cast their votes using EVMs in a secure environment.

7

Vote Counting

Fixed Date

Votes are counted under strict surveillance of ECI officials and candidate representatives.

8

Declaration of Result

Immediate

Successful candidates are announced and the formal notification completes the election process.
